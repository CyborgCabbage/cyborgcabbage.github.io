This was my entry for the GMTK 2022 game jam. 

The theme was "Roll of the Dice", the way I interpreted that was to make a game where you control a die with your numeric keys (1 to 6). As long as a key is pressed the corresponding face of the dice will emit a "thrust" which propels the dice in the opposite direction. Conceptually it is a fusion of Outer Wilds and QWOP controls.

It is definitely a rage game like Getting Over It or I Am Bread, though I wouldn't put it in the same league in terms of design. I think the primary issue with my design is that if you hit something and the dice rotates the game becomes much harder, so rotating at all becomes the fail state. The winning strategy is to restart every time the dies orientation gets perturbed, and that simplifies the game too much. It also creates a layer of disconnect in the design, between how the game works and how it is optimally played.

It does get me thinking though: perhaps I could redo this movement scheme in a new game, maybe have the dice be in a mech or car that is moving on the ground. If movement is on the ground then that locks down the orientation and means the player doesn't have to constantly contend with gravity, though having taken care of those annoyances you would have to build the difficulty up in some other way.

One neat little thing about this game is that it uses music I made myself in [Bosca Ceoil](https://terrycavanagh.itch.io/bosca-ceoil). I am not really a musician, but I quite like these tracks.
